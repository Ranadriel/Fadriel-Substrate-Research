# The Fuckery That Is Fuck

*A word analysis.* Collected by Rana. Second satirical companion to [The Mad Science of Nothing](../mad_science_of_nothing/), from the same Institute of Negative Space (Department of Lexical Forensics).

**Goal: find all the fucks.**

## Open

- [`The Fuckery That Is Fuck.html`](./The%20Fuckery%20That%20Is%20Fuck.html): the analysis, in nineteen specimens.
- [`The Atlas of Fuck.html`](./The%20Atlas%20of%20Fuck.html): the census, as a map. 1,203 recorded senses, compounds, phrases, acronyms and disguises, dated to their first appearance in writing wherever the record allows and sorted into thirteen territories of meaning. A year slider runs from 1300 to now; press play to watch one word for one act spread across the language.

Both are single self-contained pages with no build step. They work offline apart from web fonts.

## The thesis

The meaning of this word is the least important thing about it. Each specimen separates the word's *meaning* from its *force*, and each time the force turns out to live somewhere else:

| Experiment | Result |
|---|---|
| Delete the letters (f\*\*\*, "the F-word") | Meaning stays; the measurable physical response drops |
| Delete the whole word ([expletive deleted]) | The gap fills with worse |
| Translate it (*Fack ju Göhte*) | Meaning travels; the sting stays home |
| Invent a stand-in (*fouch*, *twizpipe*) | No effect on pain tolerance |
| Overuse it | The charge drains |
| Destroy the language (aphasia) | Meaning goes; the swear stays |
| Outlaw it | The court grabs air |
| Ban it from print (1857–1960s) | It multiplies underground: 15 senses by 1700, 55 by 1900 |
| Say it constantly (the 1914–18 trenches) | It means nothing; leaving it out signals danger |
| Rule that it only means sex (FCC, 2004) | 901 of 1,203 recorded senses live outside the Carnal Lands |
| Shrink it to an asterisk | It still delivers the whole word |

That is the via negativa: a hollow word with perfect structure.

## The Atlas: method

The census was built in five passes; the full paper trail is in [`CENSUS_AUDIT.md`](./CENSUS_AUDIT.md).

1. **The headword walk.** Each Green's entry links to the next in alphabetical order. Starting at *fuck, n.*, the survey followed those links one entry at a time to *fuck you!*: all 73 headwords that begin with the word, every numbered sense of each.
2. **The search sweep.** Green's search for *fuck* reports 277 results but displays only 30 at a time. Split by the letter after the word (*fucka* … *fuckz*), each slice displayed in full and matched Green's own count; together they list exactly the 277. Every result is accounted for: 250 counted, 20 cross-references to entries already counted, 7 spelling variants folded in, 0 missing.
3. **The second-word sweep.** Green's search only matches the start of a headword, so words where *fuck* comes second (*eyefuck*, *get fucked*, *Bumfuck, Egypt*, *absofuckinglutely*) were found by searching every plausible first element: kin, body parts, animals, emotions, nationalities, phrase openers, infixed words, and every relative named in the 277. Hits and misses are both logged. A second round, run on every second-word fuck in Wiktionary's list, found 32 more of Green's (the *fiddle-fuck* family, *tucker-fucker*, *bull fuck*, *sweet Fanny Adams*), and listing all 37 of Green's *not give a…* results added *not give a frig* and *not give a 4X*.
4. **The disguises.** Stand-ins from other sources fill out the Fog of Euphemism.
5. **The Wiktionary check.** Every one of the 640 single words containing *fuck* in Wiktionary's English dictionary (October 2026, 936,599 words), and every one of its 5,061 vulgar senses, was checked against the census. The 453 fucks Wiktionary records that Green's doesn't are now in it, from *fuckwaffle* and *fuck trophy* to a hundred acronyms and the oldest find of the survey, Florio's 1598 *windfucker*, the kestrel. Pronunciation spellings, other languages' words for fuck, and words that only use it in a definition are listed in the audit and not counted.

Each numbered sense counts as its own fuck. Spelling variants are folded together and pure cross-references aren't double-counted. Dates are earliest citations, so they mark when a sense was first written down, not first said; where another source dates something earlier than Green's, the earlier date is used. Glosses are original.

**The undated.** 530 fucks are counted without a date; the Atlas shows them at the present day. 84 are Green's: its longest entries (*fuck, n.*, *fuck, v.*, and a few others) run past what the survey's tools can load, and Green's search index, which lists every sub-entry past the cut, gives glosses but no dates. One (*fsck*) is FOLDOC's. The other 445 are Wiktionary's: found, counted and mapped, with their dates still to be read from each word's own page.

The thirteen territories, in the order they were settled: the Carnal Lands, the Fog of Euphemism, the Intensifier Sea, the Placeholder Plains (the windfucker, 1598), the Insult Badlands, the Void, the Dismissal Coast, the Ruinlands, the Exclamation Peaks, the Warm Springs, the Idle Marshes, the Altered States, and the Acronym Archipelago.

## Rigor

The register is satire; the content is not. Every dated or numbered claim is sourced in the page's bibliography. Findings that are disputed are marked *contested* in the text rather than smoothed over: the right-hemisphere theory of swearing, the Phineas Gage profanity legend, the 2009 heart-rate result, and the first-on-British-TV claim.
