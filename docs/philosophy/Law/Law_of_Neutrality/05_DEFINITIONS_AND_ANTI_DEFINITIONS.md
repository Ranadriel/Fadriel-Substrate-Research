`[Ranadriel, 2026-10-22]` The article needs its own acts and definitions — and **anti-definitions: what each term is *not*.**

Every term below has two parts. **Means** says what it is. **Is not** closes the doors the house has used to stretch or shrink it.

---

## Hard Questions First

**HQ-A. Expressio unius.** *A list of what a term is not invites the court to infer that anything unlisted is allowed.*
Answer: Rule of Construction 3 forbids any inference from omission. `[Wounded]` — the rule itself must survive interpretation.

**HQ-B. Renaming.** *Exclude a doctrine by name and the house renames it.* It already has, three times: "good faith and probable cause" (*Pierson v. Ray*, 1967); "objective legal reasonableness" measured against "clearly established" law (*Harlow v. Fitzgerald*, 1982); the right's contours "beyond debate" (*Ashcroft v. al-Kidd*, 2011). Same shield, new names.
Answer: **Anti-define by function, not by name.** "Immunity" is defined by what a rule *does*, "however named" (§ 1.12 and Rule 2). A renamed shield is still caught. `[Defeated, if § 1.12 holds]`

**HQ-C. Breadth.** *"No official" reaches legislators, whose speech and debate the Constitution itself protects (Art. I, § 6).*
Answer: `[Open]` — the article either amends that protection for injury claims or carves legislators out. A new front either way.

## Section 1 — Definitions

### § 1.1 "Official"
**Means** every person who exercises any power of the United States, of a State, or of any political subdivision of either, whether by election, appointment, employment, or contract.
**Is not**
- limited to employees;
- narrowed by the person's title, rank, or office;
- suspended while the person is off duty, if they act under color of office (§ 1.3).

### § 1.2 "Law-enforcement official"
**Means** any official who holds a power of arrest, or who carries a weapon by government authority.
**Is not** narrowed to exclude contractors, reserve or auxiliary officers, campus, transit, or corrections officers.

### § 1.3 "Under color of office"
**Means** acting while on duty, or using a power, badge, uniform, weapon, vehicle, record, or access that the person holds only by reason of office.
**Is not** conditioned on the act being
- authorized by any law (*this closes Render D's authorization gate*);
- within the scope of employment;
- for the benefit of the employing government.

### § 1.4 "Violation"
**Means** an act or omission of an official, under color of office, that
- (a) breaches a duty stated in this article; or
- (b) deprives a person of a right secured by this Constitution.

**Is not** conditioned on
- a finding that the act was unreasonable, for path (a);
- any court having previously held similar conduct unlawful;
- the official knowing the conduct was unlawful;
- the conduct being a crime, or on any prosecution or conviction.

### § 1.5 "Injury"
**Means** any of the following, caused by a violation or by an act under color of office:
- death;
- bodily injury;
- loss of liberty;
- loss of, damage to, or destruction of property, **including an animal**;
- `[Open]` emotional, monetary, and associative harm, as Render D listed them — each to be defined if retained.

**Is not**
- limited to physical harm;
- conditioned on the conduct being a crime.

### § 1.6 "Animal"
**Means** any animal kept by a person.
**Is not** valued as property alone. Damages for the injury or death of an animal are not limited to its market value.

### § 1.7 "Knowledge"
**Means** an official knows a fact when
- (a) they are aware of it; or
- (b) **deemed knowledge:** the fact was stated in their presence and is audible or visible on their own recording device, or on any recording in their employer's custody.

**Is not**
- proof of what the official believed about the law;
- deemed for a fact not audible or visible on the recording;
- `[Open]` deemed for a statement in a language the official does not speak.

### § 1.8 The four states of mind

| Term | Means | Is not |
|---|---|---|
| **Accident** | An injury caused under color of office that follows from **no violation**. | An injury is not an accident because the official did not intend it, did not foresee it, or acted quickly. |
| **Negligence** | A violation committed **without knowledge** of the facts that make it a violation. | Not excused by workload, haste, or the official's training. |
| **Recklessness** | A violation committed **with knowledge** of the facts that make it a violation. | Does not require knowing the act was unlawful, or intending the injury. |
| **Intent** | A violation committed **for the purpose** of causing the injury. | Not excused by any belief that the injury was deserved. |

*No degree words — "substantial," "unjustifiable," "gross" — appear in these definitions. That is deliberate.*

### § 1.9 "Self-report"
**Means** a written report by the official that
- names the date, place, and persons affected, and the official's own act;
- is delivered to `[the designated office]` within **[10] days** of the act; and
- is delivered **before any trigger event** (§ 1.10).

**Is not**
- a report delivered after a trigger event;
- a report made for the official by another person;
- a report that omits the official's own act;
- a use-of-force report required by an employer, unless it meets every element above;
- available for an act that is a crime;
- available more than **[once in N years]**.

### § 1.10 "Trigger event"
**Means** the earliest of:
- the filing of a complaint, claim, or notice of claim naming the incident;
- a records request naming the incident;
- public release of any recording of the incident;
- notice to the official of any investigation of the incident.

**Is not** delayed by the official's lack of knowledge of the event. `[Open]`

### § 1.11 "Evidence deadline"
**Means**
- a recording is preserved within **[24 hours]** of the incident;
- physical evidence is logged within **[24 hours]**;
- a recording is produced to the injured person or their counsel within **[30] days** of a written request.

**Is not**
- extended by workload or backlog;
- `[Open]` extended by the pendency of any investigation.

**Charged to:** the employing government for systemic delay; the official personally only where the evidence was in their custody and the deadline passed without action.

### § 1.12 "Immunity"
**Means** any rule, **however named**, that bars, defeats, delays, or limits liability or suit under this article because of
- (a) the official's title, office, or role;
- (b) the official's belief in the lawfulness of the act;
- (c) the absence of a prior decision addressing similar facts;
- (d) the sovereign character of the employing government; or
- (e) any doctrine of the common law.

**Includes by name:** qualified immunity; the good-faith defense; absolute judicial immunity, except for a judicial act (§ 1.13); sovereign immunity; the policy-or-custom requirement; `[Open]` absolute prosecutorial immunity.

**Is not** the protection this article itself grants: the self-report protection (§ 1.9) and the judicial-act protection (§ 1.13).

### § 1.13 "Judicial act"
**Means** an act of a judge that
- (a) bears a docket number assigned at or before the act;
- (b) is in writing or on a transcribed record; and
- (c) is reviewable by appeal, by motion, or at a hearing set.

**Is not**
- an act missing any of the three;
- an administrative or employment act;
- judicial because it is of a kind normally performed by a judge (*against Stump*);
- judicial because the judge had general jurisdiction over the subject (*against Bradley*);
- judicial because it was done in a courtroom or from the bench.

### § 1.14 "Employing government"
**Means** the United States, a State, or a political subdivision of either, on whose behalf the official acted.
**Is not**
- relieved of liability for want of a policy or custom (*against Monell*);
- protected by sovereign immunity of any kind (*against Hans and Will*);
- `[Open]` protected by any damages cap below **[N]**.

## Rules of Construction

1. **No implied immunity.** No immunity shall be implied into this article.
2. **However named.** A rule excluded by this article stays excluded under any other name.
3. **No inference from omission.** The listing of any item in this article, or its omission from a list, implies nothing about any item not listed.
4. **No balancing.** Where this article states a rule, no court shall weigh it against any interest the article does not state.
5. **Words and definitions control.** This article is read by its words and the definitions in this section. No common-law background, structural inference, or claim of sovereignty narrows it.
6. **Ambiguity resolves toward the remedy.**
